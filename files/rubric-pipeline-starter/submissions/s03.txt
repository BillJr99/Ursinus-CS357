The town should build protected bike lanes along Main Street rather than keep the painted lanes it has now. Paint does not stop a delivery truck from parking in the lane, and the traffic office logged an average of six blocked-lane complaints per week last fall. A curb or a row of planters does stop the truck, which is why the two blocks that already have barriers reported zero blockages over the same period. Business owners worry about losing parking, but the plan removes only nine spaces while adding a rack that holds thirty bikes. Protected lanes cost more than paint up front, and they are the only version that riders will actually trust.
