The dining hall should stay open until 9 p.m. on weeknights instead of closing at 7. The current schedule shuts out the students who need dinner most. Evening labs and studio courses run until 7:50, so anyone in those sections misses the last hot meal three nights a week. Last spring's student government survey found that most of the students who skipped dinner named the closing time, not the food, as the reason. Some argue that a later close costs money, but the hall already keeps a dish crew until 9 to finish cleanup, so the added expense is mostly food, not labor. A two-hour extension is a small change that keeps students fed on the nights they have the least time to cook.
