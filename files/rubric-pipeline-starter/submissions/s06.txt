A campus shuttle to the train station would make it easier for students to get home on weekends. Students without cars now depend on rides from friends or on a taxi that costs more than the train ticket itself. A shuttle that runs on Friday afternoon and Sunday evening would cover the times when most people travel. Other colleges in the area do this already, which shows it is possible. The main objection is cost, however a shuttle could charge a small fare to help pay for the driver. This would be a helpful service for a lot of students.
