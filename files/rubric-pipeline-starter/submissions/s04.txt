The library should extend its hours during finals week. Many students study late and the library closes at midnight, so they have to move to the dorm lounges which are noisy. My roommate and I both had to leave the library on the last night of the semester before we finished studying. I think a 24 hour schedule for one week would not cost that much. Some people would say students should just plan better, but exams pile up in the same week no matter how you plan. Extending the hours would help students do their best on their exams.
