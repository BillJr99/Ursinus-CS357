The dining hall should start composting its food waste. Right now everything that gets scraped off a plate goes into the same bins as the plastic and paper. Composting would reduce the amount that goes to the landfill. The environmental club ran a one-day bin at lunch last year and filled three buckets with scraps from a single meal period, so the volume is definitely there. It would take some training for the staff and the students, and that is the main reason it hasn't happened yet. Overall composting is a good idea and the school should do it.
