Whisk one cup of flour, one tablespoon of sugar, two teaspoons of baking powder, and a pinch of salt in a large bowl. In a second bowl, beat one egg with one cup of milk and two tablespoons of melted butter. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir until just combined; a few lumps are fine. Heat a skillet over medium heat and pour a quarter cup of batter for each pancake. Flip when bubbles form on the surface, cook one more minute, and serve warm with syrup or fruit.
