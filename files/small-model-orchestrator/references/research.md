# Research Reliability Protocol

## Contract

Define:
- research question;
- scope;
- date/freshness requirements;
- source-quality requirements;
- disputed terms;
- expected synthesis.

## Source discipline

Prefer primary sources for factual claims and specifications. Use multiple independent sources for consequential or contested claims when possible.

Record:
- source;
- date;
- exact claim supported;
- source quality;
- conflicts.

## Verification

Before finalizing:
- distinguish source fact from inference;
- resolve date ambiguity;
- check whether sources refer to the same version/entity;
- search for counterevidence;
- identify unsupported claims;
- avoid turning absence of evidence into evidence of absence.

## Gauntlet

Ask:
- What source would falsify the conclusion?
- Are citations actually supporting the adjacent claims?
- Is anything stale?
- Is the source circularly citing another summary?
- Are there material perspectives or primary records missing?
