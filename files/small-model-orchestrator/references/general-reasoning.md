# General Reasoning Reliability Protocol

For tasks without strong external tools, manufacture structure and falsification opportunities rather than relying on one-pass intuition.

## Procedure

1. Translate the question into explicit claims/subproblems.
2. Record assumptions.
3. Derive candidate answer.
4. Independently re-derive using a different representation or method where feasible.
5. Search for counterexamples.
6. Check boundary cases.
7. Verify arithmetic symbolically or computationally if tools exist.
8. Separate certainty about premises from certainty about inference.
9. Report unresolved ambiguity.

## Cold critic

Give the critic the problem statement and proposed result, but not the builder's rationale first. Ask the critic to derive its own answer or identify a counterexample. Compare afterward.
