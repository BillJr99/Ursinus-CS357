  rubric:
    - weight: 40
      description: "Progress Report: shipped, next, and current risks"
      preemerging: "The report is missing, or it has no specific content."
      beginning: "All three sections are present, but they stay general, with no named work or risk."
      progressing: "Each section names specific work or risks, though a risk has no plan or an item has no owner."
      proficient: "Each section is specific and honest: it names what shipped, what is next and who owns it, and each risk with what the team will do about it."
    - weight: 30
      description: "Stage Prompts: the three prompts for this check-in"
      preemerging: "The prompts are not answered."
      beginning: "One or two prompts are answered, or the answers could describe any team."
      progressing: "All three prompts are answered with something that happened on this team."
      proficient: "All three prompts are answered with concrete examples, and at least one answer says what the team will change or keep because of it."
    - weight: 30
      description: "Signatures and Pulse: every member signed, and every member sent a private pulse"
      preemerging: "No signatures, and no pulses."
      beginning: "Some members signed or sent a pulse."
      progressing: "Every member signed, but a pulse is missing or late."
      proficient: "Every member signed the report, and every member submitted a pulse on time."
