title: "CS357: Foundations of Artificial Intelligence - Literature Review, Phase 1 Rubric"

info:
  coursenum: CS357
  purpose: "To check that each team member has opened, read, and annotated real sources before the team writes its synthesis."
  tilt:
    task: "Complete Phase 1 of the Literature Review: an individual annotated bibliography of 2-3 sources."
    criteria: "This is a completion check.  I grade the quality of your annotations with the full Literature Review rubric when the team submits Phase 2."
  points: 3
  goals:
    - To research the team's stakeholder problem through published sources, assessing each source's credibility and relevance to the problem statement (Goal 11)
  rubric:
    - weight: 60
      description: Annotations Complete
      preemerging: No annotations are submitted
      beginning: Annotations are copied abstracts, or fewer than two sources are annotated
      progressing: 2-3 sources are annotated, but at least one annotation is missing its summary, credibility assessment, or relevance statement
      proficient: Each of the 2-3 annotations has a summary in your own words, a credibility assessment, and a relevance statement
    - weight: 40
      description: Sources and Citations
      preemerging: No sources can be identified or opened
      beginning: Sources are listed, but no source is scholarly, or a link or DOI does not resolve
      progressing: At least one source is scholarly and every source opens, but a citation is incomplete or a how-I-found-it line is missing
      proficient: At least one source is scholarly, every link or DOI resolves, and every source has a complete citation and a how-I-found-it line
